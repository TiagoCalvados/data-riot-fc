# SDW brand assets

The original club artwork is used without redrawing or retouching it.

- `sdw-logo.jpg`: the red-and-white crest published by SDW's own `@ascSDW`
  club profile, downloaded unchanged from
  https://pbs.twimg.com/profile_images/1466159860052074507/FcecigOh_400x400.jpg
  (400 × 400 JPEG, including the original grass background).
- Club profile: https://x.com/ascSDW
- Crest cross-checked against the current official club welcome artwork:
  https://website.storage/Data/SDW/RTE/Afbeeldingen/Algemeen/mobile_welkom.png
  on https://www.ascsdw.nl/ . The old circular crest was deliberately not used.
- `sdw-favicon.ico`: the original favicon from the SDW webshop linked by the club:
  https://www.clubproducten.nl/SDW/img/favicon.ico?1705699923
  (source page https://www.clubproducten.nl/SDW/).

Source assets retrieved 5 November 2026. SDW retains rights in its crest.
Application icons are proportional size variants of the same source image.
The maskable icon places that image within the central safe area on a green canvas.
Rebuild the derived icons using `scripts/build_icons.ps1`.
